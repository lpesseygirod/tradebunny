Forecasts: who is calibrated?

Each member makes two or three checkable forecasts per meeting; code scores them

A good forecaster's 70% calls come true about 70% of the time. The Brier score measures that: 0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip, higher is worse than guessing. Once a member has 5 scored forecasts, the CIO sees their record before every decision.
